Campus Fraud

Inside the hostel mess scam: ₹2.4 crore unaccounted across 6 colleges

A six-month investigation across hostels in three states reveals a pattern of falsified mess registers, ghost tenders, and missing audit trails — and how students have been paying for it.

AK
Ananya Krishnan
DU North Campus
📅 JULY 26, 2026
⏱ 8 MIN READ
SHARE STORY:
A hostel mess register obtained during the six-month WEVOX investigation in Lucknow.

It started with a simple complaint. A first-year student at a prominent Delhi University college noticed her mess register had been signed for breakfast on a day she'd been home with a fever. When she pointed it out, the warden told her the system was "automatic." It wasn't.

Over the next six months, WEVOX correspondents pulled mess registers, vendor invoices, and audit notes from six colleges across three states. What we found is not a single rogue contractor. It's a pattern.

The numbers that don't add up

At three of the six colleges examined, the registered student count for mess attendance exceeded the actual hostel occupancy by between 11% and 23%. In rupees, that's a delta of ₹2.4 crore across one academic year — money billed to administrations for meals that were never served.

"The signatures on the register are real. The students are real. They just weren't there that day."

That quote, from a former kitchen supervisor at one of the colleges, sits at the heart of the scheme. Mess contractors aren't inventing fake students. They're over-counting real ones — using stamps, photocopied signatures, and end-of-month "catch up" entries when no one is watching.
